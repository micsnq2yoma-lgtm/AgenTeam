# Data Processing Addendum Summary

## Sub-Processor Notice & Objection Window

The Processor maintains a public registry of authorized sub-processors and must notify the Controller at least 30 calendar days before onboarding any new sub-processor that handles personal data. The Controller may raise reasonable privacy objections in writing within 15 calendar days of receiving notice; if no mitigation is agreed, the Controller may terminate affected services without penalty and receive a prorated refund.

## Security Incident Notification

Upon confirming a personal data breach affecting Customer Data, the Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after confirmation, providing the incident timeline, affected record categories, and containment steps already executed.
