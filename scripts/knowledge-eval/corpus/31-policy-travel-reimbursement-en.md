# Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Receipt Submission & Payout Window

Employees traveling on approved company business must upload itemized digital receipts through the finance portal within 14 calendar days of returning from the trip. Approved claims are settled via payroll direct deposit within 10 business days after manager and finance sign-off; submissions filed more than 45 days late require VP exception approval.

## Airfare & Lodging Allowances

Economy class is standard for all flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted on international legs exceeding 6 continuous flight hours. Daily meal per diem is capped at USD 75 domestically and USD 105 internationally without alcohol charges.
